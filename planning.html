01 · Segmentation

ABC-XYZ matrix

ABC ranks SKUs by annual consumption value (Pareto). XYZ ranks them by demand variability (coefficient of variation of monthly demand). Together they decide where to spend planning attention and how much buffer each SKU needs. Click a cell to filter the table.

03 · Order size

Economic order quantity

04 · Forecast quality

Accuracy and bias

Accuracy and bias answer different questions. MAPE says how far off the forecast is on average; bias says whether it is consistently too high or too low. A forecast can have acceptable MAPE and still overbuild inventory every month. The tracking signal flags bias that has persisted long enough to act on.
